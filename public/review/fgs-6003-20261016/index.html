Why the permission is needed

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE is used for continuous data transfer to an external OBD adapter. A user explicitly starts the diagnostic session. AutoMind exchanges OBD requests and measurement responses while that session is active. Deferring this work or interrupting the adapter connection would create gaps in the live measurements requested by the user.

Steps shown in the video

  1. 00:00–00:34: Open the OBD connection screen, enter the Wi-Fi adapter address, tap Connect and show supported live RPM. The fixture supplies a fixed 800 rpm; unsupported speed remains unavailable.
  2. 00:34–00:54: Go to Android Home. The ongoing connected-device service supports continued adapter polling in the background.
  3. 00:54–01:22: Show the ongoing “AutoMind vehicle monitoring” notification. Tap Disconnect. The notification disappears; local service inspection and fixture logs confirm the OBD foreground service and requests stop.
  4. 01:22–01:32: State the scope and remaining physical-device checks.

Validation and limits

The captured fixture trace contains one TCP client and 119 RPM samples over approximately four minutes. During the sampled background interval, RPM requests continued at approximately two-second intervals. This is a short emulator demonstration, not a real-car or long-duration reliability test.

Screen-off operation, Bluetooth and behavior on physical phones still require testing with an actual adapter and parked vehicle. CarPlay and Android Auto are disabled in build 6003. This demonstration does not claim approval for either car platform.
